Privacy Policy

Among Us Codes Bot · Effective date: 17 October 2026

Policy home
In short: The bot may process Discord identifiers, command content, and configuration or feature data needed to provide its Among Us code/community features.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Discord identifiers: server, user, channel, role, and message IDs when needed to operate commands, configure the bot, or prevent duplicate processing.
  • Display information: usernames and display names where needed to respond to commands or identify who submitted content.
  • Command and submitted content: command text, Among Us room codes, and related messages that are needed to provide the bot's features.
  • Configuration and technical data: server settings and diagnostic/error information needed to maintain the bot.

2. How information is used

Information is used to respond to commands, provide code/community features, maintain server configuration, prevent abuse or duplicate processing, and troubleshoot reliability issues.

3. Storage and retention

Depending on enabled features, settings or other records may be stored so they persist across restarts. Content may remain in Discord channels according to the server's own settings and retention practices. Stored bot data is retained only as needed to operate the features, maintain configuration, prevent abuse, or troubleshoot issues, and may be deleted when no longer needed.

4. Sharing

The bot owner does not sell personal information. Information posted in a Discord channel may be visible to members who can access that channel. Information may be processed by Discord and the bot's hosting provider under their own policies, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service.

5. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the bot or ask a server administrator to remove it. For requests concerning data stored by the bot, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route and provide the server and relevant details. Data in Discord messages may need to be deleted by the author or a server moderator using Discord's available controls.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information through the bot.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version here. For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route provided with the bot.
