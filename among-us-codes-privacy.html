Privacy Policy

Among Us Link · Effective date: 17 October 2026

Policy home
In short: Among Us Link may process Discord identifiers, command content, lobby codes, and server configuration needed to provide its features. The exact data processed depends on the features enabled and how the bot is configured.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Discord identifiers: server, user, channel, role, and message IDs when needed to respond to commands, configure the bot, or prevent duplicate processing.
  • Display information: usernames and display names where needed to identify a command user or display who submitted a lobby.
  • Lobby and command content: command text, lobby codes, host or lobby details, and related messages needed to provide the bot's features.
  • Server settings: configuration selected by server administrators, such as channels or options used by the bot.
  • Technical data: error logs and diagnostic information needed to troubleshoot problems, maintain reliability, and prevent abuse.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to commands, share or organize lobby information, maintain server configuration, prevent duplicate processing or abuse, and diagnose technical issues.

3. Storage and retention

Depending on the features enabled, settings or lobby records may be stored so they persist across bot restarts. Messages posted in Discord may remain visible according to the server's channel permissions and Discord's own retention practices. Bot-stored data is retained only as needed to operate features, maintain configuration, prevent abuse, or troubleshoot issues, and may be deleted when no longer needed.

4. Sharing and visibility

The bot owner does not sell personal information. Content submitted to a Discord channel may be visible to members who can access that channel. Information may be processed by Discord and the hosting provider used to operate the bot, under their own policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Third-party services

Among Us Link relies on Discord to operate. Discord handles information under its own privacy policy. The bot is not operated by or affiliated with Discord, Innersloth, or Among Us unless explicitly stated.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using Among Us Link or ask a server administrator to remove it. For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning data stored by the bot, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route and include the server and relevant record details. Some data may need to be retained temporarily for operation, security, duplicate prevention, or legal reasons. Content already posted in Discord may need to be removed by its author or a server moderator using Discord's available controls.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through the bot.

8. Children

Among Us Link is not designed to collect sensitive personal information from children. Users should follow Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version on this page. For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route provided with Among Us Link.
