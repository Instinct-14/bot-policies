Privacy Policy

Omen · Effective date: 17 October 2026

Policy home
In short: Omen processes Discord identifiers and the information needed to operate the features used in a server. Server configuration and feature records may be stored so they persist between restarts.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Discord identifiers: server, user, channel, role, and message IDs, where needed for commands, configuration, records, or duplicate prevention.
  • Display information: usernames, display names, and role information needed to identify participants or manage personal roles.
  • Feature data: streak turns and outcomes, recorded amounts and timestamps; event-payment records such as payer, host, credited amount, channel and time; booster-role ownership, appearance, sharing settings and pending removal deadlines.
  • Command and message data: messages or command content that the bot needs to respond to, detect supported transaction confirmations, or run a requested feature.
  • Technical data: error and diagnostic information needed to maintain reliability and prevent duplicate processing.

2. How information is used

Information is used to run commands, maintain records requested by server members or administrators, enforce feature settings, manage booster roles and deadlines, prevent duplicate processing, troubleshoot errors, and protect the bot from abuse.

3. Storage and retention

Some records may be stored in a database so they persist across restarts. Records are retained while needed to provide the feature, maintain server history, prevent duplicates, or resolve technical issues. Retention periods can vary by feature and server configuration; records may be removed when no longer needed or when a valid deletion request can be fulfilled.

4. Sharing

Omen does not sell personal information. Feature records may be visible to people who have permission to use the relevant bot commands or view the configured Discord channel. Information may be processed by Discord and by the hosting provider used to operate the bot, subject to their own policies. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Messages and transaction detection

Where a server enables transaction tracking, Omen may inspect relevant bot responses or messages in configured channels to identify confirmed transactions and record the resulting feature data. It should not be treated as a financial service or as the authoritative source for a payment.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop using Omen or ask a server administrator to remove it. For access, correction, or deletion requests about stored records, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route and include the server and relevant record details. Some records may need to be retained temporarily for operation, security, duplicate prevention, or legal reasons.

7. Security and children

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Server administrators should not use Omen to store sensitive personal information. The bot is not designed to collect sensitive personal data from children.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated by publishing a revised version here. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the official support/contact route provided with Omen.
